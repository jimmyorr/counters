About Counters

A minimalist score keeping application

Privacy policy

Counters is a clean, modern score tracker designed to replace pen and paper for tabletop games, board games, habit tracking, and more. With responsive controls, persistent local storage, and built-in gaming tools, it makes keeping track of values seamless.

Frequently asked questions

1. Where is my data stored?

All your counters, history log entries, and custom layouts are saved completely locally on your device. We do not use external cloud servers for your data, meaning your scores are private and always available offline.

2. How do I delete or edit a counter?

Tap the menu button or the counter settings icon on any individual card to edit its name, change its color, reset its score, or delete it entirely.
